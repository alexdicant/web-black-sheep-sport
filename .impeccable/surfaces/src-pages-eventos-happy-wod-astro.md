# HAPPY WOD event gallery

Mode: Operate. Scope: `/eventos/happy-wod/` only. The athlete must identify and select photos quickly on a phone, then continue to WhatsApp. Keep the production home intact except for the HAPPY WOD CTA in this feature branch.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The photos are the interface. A compact event heading gives way immediately to a dense, calm selection grid; the page refuses a storefront shell, heavy cards, and commercial pricing logic.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit Black Sheep Sport exactly: white and cool-gray surfaces, cold ink, volt only for active state and action, Archivo Expanded headings, Instrument Sans UI, 2px radii, square photographic frames, crisp one-pixel structure.

STORY: The athlete sees HAPPY WOD, understands tap-to-select, recognizes chosen photos through border plus check plus announced state, can clear mistakes, and sends the current ordered codes to WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Existing compact Header, a short event block with name, venue/date and two instruction lines, followed immediately by a two-column photo grid. No large hero. The purchase action appears only after the first selection as a safe-area-aware fixed bottom bar.

FORM: Direct extension of the established Operate surface, shaped from the user's precise specification; seed key: not-run-precisely-specified. Signature interaction: a photo button changes from neutral to volt-accented with an explicit check while the bottom action bar reports the live count.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
